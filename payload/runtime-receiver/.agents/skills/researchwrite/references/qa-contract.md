# Four-layer QA contract

## 1. Evidence gate

- Every material factual claim maps to supplied evidence or is clearly labeled.
- No invented reference, result, number, method completion, or feasibility claim.
- Causal and novelty wording does not exceed the available support.

## 2. Argument gate

- The gap leads to one explicit research question.
- The thesis answers that question and is supported by the proposed work.
- Alternatives, limitations, and failure conditions are not hidden.

## 3. Section-contract gate

- Each section has one primary purpose and uses only allowed claims.
- Methods can test the stated question; outcomes do not presuppose success.
- Terminology, units, symbols, and abbreviations remain consistent.

## 4. Prose and deliverable gate

- Paragraphs have a clear claim-evidence-meaning function.
- Transitions preserve the argument rather than merely sounding fluent.
- Requested artifacts exist, placeholders are visible, and unperformed validation
  is disclosed.

Record failures, required fixes, and a stop/continue recommendation in
`templates/quality-check.md`.
